## Brand & Style

This design system defines an enterprise-grade Agricultural Decision Support System (ADSS) built for precision agronomy, crop yield forecasting, soil biome analysis, and automated irrigation management across the Indian agricultural belt.

The aesthetic philosophy bridges the calculated precision of modern financial infrastructure (Stripe), the human-centered clarity of premium consumer operating systems (Apple), and the systematic density of enterprise software (Microsoft Azure/AgTech). The design style is **Corporate / Modern** merged with **Tactile Data Density**:
- **Demeanor:** Quiet, scientific, authoritative, and deeply grounded. It avoids folkloric clichés, ornamental textures, and chaotic dashboard syndrome in favor of calm, high-legibility telemetry and explainable intelligence.
- **Target Audience:** Agronomists, agricultural bank risk officers, farm cluster directors, crop insurance actuaries, and progressive rural enterprise operators.
- **Emotional Intent:** Instilling complete confidence in mission-critical agronomic recommendations (e.g., nitrogen scheduling, micro-climate frost alerts, irrigation drawdown) where poor guidance directly impacts multi-acre financial livelihoods.

## Colors

The palette draws from the physiological markers of vigorous crop growth, water systems, and arable terrain, balanced with rigorous accessibility standards:

- **Primary (`#114232` - Deep Forest Canopy):** The core anchor. Conveys institutional trust, biological depth, and executive clarity. Used for primary interactive actions, high-emphasis headers, active navigational states, and key metrics.
- **Secondary (`#16A34A` - Vibrant Chlorophyll):** Used to signify healthy physiological indicators, positive yield delta, validated soil tests, and optimal moisture bands.
- **Tertiary (`#0284C7` - Irrigation Azure):** Dedicated exclusively to water-related telemetry: evapotranspiration coefficients, aquifer depth, canal flow sensors, and atmospheric humidity graphs.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#F9FAF6` - Calcined Lime & Pale Loam):** A warm, low-strain off-white surface that mitigates harsh blue-light glare in high-ambient sunlight field conditions while feeling distinctly organic compared to generic cold grays.
- **Semantic Accents:**
  - *Caution / Drought (`#D97706`):* Pests nearing economic threshold levels, localized soil moisture depletion, and moderate heat stress.
  - *Critical / Blight (`#DC2626`):* Pathogen outbreaks, canal valve failures, extreme frost warnings, and immediate crop loss interventions.
  - *Surface Borders (`#E2E8DE`):* A faint warm-sage border tone providing razor-sharp structure without visual noise.

## Typography

Typography balances humanistic clarity with tabular data efficiency:

- **Manrope** handles all display headings, body narratives, advisory text, and interface controls. Its balanced geometric construction provides modern authority without feeling sterile or robotic.
- **JetBrains Mono** is introduced intentionally as the label and telemetry engine. It handles IoT sensor telemetry (pH levels, EC conductivity, soil temperature, NDVI indexes, NPK kg/ha) and explainable AI SHAP weights, preserving numeric alignment across rapid tabular scans.
- **Tabular Figures:** All numeric displays enforce proportional figure alignment (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to prevent jittering during live satellite feeds and telemetric updates.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an architectural 12-column fluid grid system paired with a strict 4px/8px incremental rhythm:

- **Grid Architecture:** 
  - *Desktop (≥1280px):* 12 columns, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters, 32px (`2rem`) margins. Content caps at 1440px max-width for operational console panels.
  - *Tablet (768px - 1279px):* 8 columns, 16px (`1rem`) gutters, 24px (`1.5rem`) margins.
  - *Mobile (<768px):* 4 columns, 12px (`0.75rem`) gutters, 16px (`1rem`) margins.
- **Rhythm & Padding:** Dense data cards utilize `space-md` for interior padding to optimize screen real estate. Advisory and analytical long-form reports shift to `space-lg` to encourage cognitive deceleration and thorough review.
- **Reflow Protocols:** Multicolumn telemetry tables collapse into vertically stacked metric cards on viewports under 768px, pinning geospatial map anchors to the upper 40vh of the mobile viewport.

## Elevation & Depth

To maintain visual clarity under direct field lighting, the design system minimizes deep diffuse drop shadows, prioritizing **crisp structural borders** and **tinted tonal layering**:

- **Low-Elevation Plane (Level 0 - Base):** The root canvas `#F9FAF6` sits directly beneath all surface cards.
- **Surface Layer (Level 1 - Cards & Telemetry Panels):** Pristine white (`#FFFFFF`) bound by a 1px solid structural border (`#E2E8DE`). A faint, cool-soil ambient tint (`0 1px 2px rgba(17, 66, 50, 0.04)`) separates the card from the off-white canvas.
- **Floating Overlays (Level 2 - Filter Drawers, SHAP Popovers, Dropdowns):** Layered with a 1px border (`#D1D9CD`) and an ambient elevation shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(17, 66, 50, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(17, 66, 50, 0.04)`.
- **Modal Modality (Level 3 - Critical Interventions):** `#FFFFFF` surface with `0 20px 48px -8px rgba(17, 66, 50, 0.16)`, over a backdrop scrim of `#114232` tinted to 35% opacity with an 8px blur filter.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a **Rounded (Level 2)** geometry:
- **Default Elements:** 8px (`0.5rem`) radius applied to standard buttons, input controls, metric cells, select inputs, and dropdown panels.
- **Structural Containers:** 16px (`1rem`) radius applied to cards, field boundary charts, satellite map wrappers, and analytical sections.
- **Pill Tags & Status Indicators:** Fully rounded (`9999px`) for crop cycle stages, AI confidence indicators, and categorical agronomic badges.

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#114232`, text `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #0D3528`. Subtle inset highlight `inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255,255,255,0.1)`. Hover shifts to `#16533F`. Focus state displays a 2px ring offset in `#16A34A`.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** White background `#FFFFFF`, text `#114232`, border `1px solid #E2E8DE`. Hover state transitions background to `#F1F5EE`.
- **Destructive:** Background `#DC2626`, text `#FFFFFF`, hover `#B91C1C`.

### 2. Semantic Badges & Data Chips
- Badges feature monospaced tabular typography (`label-sm`), 4px horizontal padding, and full pill rounding.
- **Healthy / Optimal:** `#DCFCE7` background with `#15803D` text and `#BBF7D0` border.
- **Water / Irrigation:** `#E0F2FE` background with `#0369A1` text and `#BAE6FD` border.
- **Caution / Pest Alert:** `#FEF3C7` background with `#B45309` text and `#FDE68A` border.
- **Critical / Wilt:** `#FEE2E2` background with `#B91C1C` text and `#FECACA` border.

### 3. Inputs & Form Fields
- Crisp `#FFFFFF` fill with `1px solid #D1D9CD` border.
- Floating or persistent labels in `body-sm` (`#475569`). Focus state highlights border in `#114232` alongside a 3px ring in `rgba(17, 66, 50, 0.08)`.
- Numeric agricultural inputs (e.g., rainfall in mm, land area in hectares) pair directly with a right-aligned monospaced unit slug.

### 4. Cards & Telemetry Blocks
- Pure white container with a 1px border (`#E2E8DE`) and 16px interior padding.
- Metric cards pair a categorical label in `label-sm` with a large metric in `telemetry-metric`, accompanied by a secondary micro-trend indicator (e.g., "↑ 12% vs 5yr mean").

### 5. Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Base control 18x18px with `0.5rem` outer form factor. Unselected states render an interior `#FFFFFF` with `1.5px solid #CBD5E1`.
- Active states fill with `#114232` featuring a sharp white checkmark or center pin.

### 6. Domain-Specific Component: Explainable AI (XAI) SHAP Indicator
- Designed to demystify ADSS recommendation drivers (e.g., "Why apply 45kg Nitrogen today?").
- Displays a horizontal bipolar bar graph:
  - Base baseline centered with a `1px` dashed line (`#94A3B8`).
  - Positive impact factors (e.g., "High vegetative demand") render as emerald bars (`#16A34A`) expanding rightward.
  - Negative limiting factors (e.g., "Rain predicted in 18h") render as amber bars (`#D97706`) expanding leftward.
  - Variable labels and quantitative contribution delta display in `label-sm` with JetBrains Mono.
