Agricultural Decision Support System · ADSS

An Explainable, Multi-Source Agricultural Decision Support System

A web platform that fuses live weather forecasts, NASA climatology, global soil data and mandi market prices into one farm summary, then turns it into explainable crop, irrigation, yield and risk advice for Indian farms. Every recommendation shows the factors and data sources behind it.
